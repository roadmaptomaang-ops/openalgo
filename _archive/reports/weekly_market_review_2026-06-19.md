## 3. Key Stories of the Week

1. **US–Iran Peace Deal (June 19):** The landmark interim deal, signed in Switzerland, lifted restrictions on Strait of Hormuz traffic and sent Brent crude tumbling toward $82/bbl. Interglobe Aviation, Bajaj Finance, UltraTech, and L&T were among India's biggest beneficiaries mid-week.

2. **Accenture Guidance Cut (June 19):** Accenture cut FY revenue growth guidance and missed revenue consensus by $90 million, citing a $100 million Middle East impact. TCS, Infosys, and Tech Mahindra ADRs fell 5–8% overnight, dragging the Nifty IT index down 5%+ on Friday and wiping the week's gains in the tech space.

3. **Five-Day Bull Run Then Friday Reversal:** Nifty gained in each of the first four sessions (Monday–Thursday), closing at 24,168 on Thursday — a near-term high. Friday reversed 155 pts (0.64%) on the IT rout, showing how single-sector shocks can unwind a week of gains.

4. **Rupee Strengthens:** The INR firmed to 94.53 vs USD on Monday (up 5 paise), supported by crashing crude import costs. For India, which imports ~90% of crude oil, the peace deal is a material macro positive — lower current account deficit, lower inflation.

5. **FII Hedging Behaviour:** While FIIs sold ₹1,025 Cr in the cash segment, they also bought puts and calls simultaneously — signalling protective hedging rather than outright bearish conviction. DIIs stepped in aggressively at every dip.
