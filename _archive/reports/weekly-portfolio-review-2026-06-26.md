## 3. Key Stories of the Week

1. **Accenture Earnings Warning (Global IT Demand):** Accenture's cautious guidance on global IT spending triggered a sector-wide sell-off in Indian IT on June 23, raising questions about FY27 revenue visibility for TCS, Infosys, and HCL.
2. **India–US Trade Deal Optimism:** Growing expectations of an imminent bilateral trade agreement lifted sentiment mid-week, supporting IT, pharma, and auto exports theme.
3. **Crude Oil Plunge (~10% weekly drop):** Brent crude fell below $77/barrel — the largest weekly drop in a month — following improved shipping activity through the Strait of Hormuz. This eased inflation fears and supported rate-sensitive sectors.
4. **RBI Stance on Rates:** RBI signalled it is "premature" to discuss rate hikes and introduced fresh liquidity support measures, giving a fillip to banking and NBFC stocks.
5. **Market Holiday — Muharram (June 26):** NSE/BSE were closed Friday, compressing the trading week to four sessions (Mon–Thu).

## 7. Portfolio Reflection Prompts

1. **Did any of your holdings significantly outperform or underperform the index this week?** IT stocks underperformed sharply (−2% to −3%) while pharma and mid/smallcaps outperformed. If you hold heavy IT, consider whether the Accenture warning changes your FY27 earnings thesis — or if this is a temporary dip.

2. **Are there any positions you should reassess given this week's sector/macro developments?** Crude's 10% drop is a double-edged sword: positive for aviation, paint, and tyre companies, negative for upstream energy and OMCs. With the rupee weakening to ₹94.8/$, exporters (IT, pharma) get a tailwind — but import-heavy businesses face margin pressure. Review exposure accordingly.

3. **What is your plan for next week — are you positioned correctly for the events ahead?** US PCE and payroll data could spike volatility in global markets. Consider whether your portfolio has enough defensives if the Fed hawks re-emerge. Domestically, the trade deal narrative is a positive catalyst — check if you have meaningful exposure to sectors that benefit (IT services, pharma, engineering exports).
