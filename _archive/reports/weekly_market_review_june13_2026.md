## 2. Top Sectoral Performers

**Outperformers:**

1. **Banking (Nifty Bank +4.25%)** — The decisive breakout was driven by improving asset quality (SBI Gross NPA at a multi-year low of 1.49%), easing crude-linked inflation concerns, and a resumption of FII buying into private and PSU bank names.

2. **Energy / Oil & Gas** — Fell sharply on crude reversal; but upstream/refining names rebounded as Brent stabilized below $90, with downstream margins improving. Anticipation of Strait of Hormuz reopening lifted sentiment.

3. **Capital Goods / Defence** — BHEL's Q4 FY26 result (net profit +156% YoY, order book at all-time high of ₹2.4 lakh crore) catalysed the entire capital goods space; defence stocks attracted fresh institutional buying.

**Underperformers:**

1. **IT** — Remained under pressure as a stronger-than-expected US NFP (139K jobs vs. 85K expected) early in the week revived fears of the Fed staying higher for longer, squeezing valuation multiples on rate-sensitive tech.

2. **Pharma** — Mixed bag; no major sector-level triggers; some stock-specific pressure from US FDA observations and generic pricing headwinds.

3. **FMCG** — Defensive positioning unwound as risk appetite improved; funds rotated out of staples into banks and cyclicals.

## 3. Key Stories of the Week

1. **US-Iran Ceasefire & Crude Drop:** Progress toward a 60-day ceasefire MOU and Strait of Hormuz reopening pushed Brent down sharply from ~$97/barrel to near $80–82, removing a key macro overhang for India.

2. **US NFP Shock (Monday):** May non-farm payrolls at 139K (vs. 85K consensus) triggered a risk-off open globally, sending Nifty down 286 points at open and raising fears of a Fed rate hike.

3. **Bank Nifty Breakout:** Banking stocks led recovery across the week. SBI's Q4 FY26 results — NII of ₹44,380 crore (+4% YoY) and Gross NPA improving to 1.49% from 1.82% — restored confidence in PSU banks.

4. **Q4 FY26 Earnings Season (Final Week):** Aggregate earnings across ~650 companies showed revenues +13.7% YoY and profits +17.8% YoY — broadly above expectations. BHEL was the blockbuster, with a 156% profit jump.

5. **RBI Stance Clarity:** RBI maintained the repo rate at 5.25% with a neutral stance. With India CPI at 3.9% (May) — below the 4% median target — the market interpreted the hold as incrementally dovish for the medium term.
